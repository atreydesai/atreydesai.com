imageDescription: "Thirteen linguistic properties that were previously thought to be unique to humans, categorized into four levels from the more rudimentary to the most complex: signal features (turn-taking), token features (reference), message features (syntax), and language features (openness). A 5-step pipeline that generalizes the research process: data collection, preprocessing, sequence representation, meaning identification, and generation, with evaluation. Of 55,843 papers, 37,712 are classified as biological and 18,131 as computational or hybrid; the clearest temporal trend is the recent acceleration of computational work. Computational classification alone provides observer-side evidence; animal-side claims require receiver behavior, and reported classification accuracy, however high, cannot by itself establish an animal-side claim."
