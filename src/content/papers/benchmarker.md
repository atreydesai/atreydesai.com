imageDescription: "BenchMarker is an education-inspired toolkit using LLM judges to flag three common MCQ flaws: contamination—items appearing exactly online; shortcuts—cues in the choices that enable guessing; and writing errors—structural/grammatical issues based on a 19-rule education rubric. Across 12 benchmarks, MCQs from exam-based benchmarks are more commonly found online (45% of MMLU; 47% of TruthfulQA appears online), but they contain far fewer writing flaws; 100% of HellaSwag violates multiple writing rules. Discarding MCQs with writing errors shifts LLM rankings beyond random, by up to two positions: Gemini 2.5 Pro goes from 3 to 1, Claude 4.5 Sonnet from 2 to 4."
