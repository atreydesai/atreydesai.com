I know for some of the earlier stuff before I created this website, I had auto-generated summaries but I felt this piece really deserved a proper summary. Carolyn was a fellow high school journalist and friend of a friend of mine, and I was always impressed by her work and her ability to express her thoughts so articulately. I though this piece in particular was one of her best works. She reflects on her experiences as a woman in Computer Science at UC Berkeley, navigating the "double-edged sword" of diversity initiatives. I particuarly appreciated the depth of her critique of microaggressions that undermine women's achievements while acknowledging the complex necessity of creating supportive communities in male-dominated fields.
