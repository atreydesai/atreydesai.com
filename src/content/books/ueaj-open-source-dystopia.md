I think my thoughts on this can be summarized as concentrated, morally confident, and unaccountable power is never the good option.

"This section is not to argue in favor of this world in the absolute sense. I despise it deeply and will fight relentlessly to prevent its inception. However, if given the choice between having people's autonomy stripped away by neglect or antisocial crises and having it stripped away by an otherwise moral authority like religion, what would you prefer?"

This is easy for the author to say given their background, but I would be interested in what non-privileged groups would respond to this hypothetical.

"This in my opinion is the strongest argument against the open source dream. AI is leverage. It empowers those that already have the cards. The centralized future is likely already set in stone, and the only thing that might stop it is policy action, which OS companies don't seem to have any interest in either."

Also, if AI capability remains exclusively inside a handful of closed labs, wouldn't governments only need to capture or coerce a few choke points to get surveillance and weapons capabilities? We are already seeing this with Anthropic, which is using Mythos to develop cyberoffensive weaponry on behalf of the US government. There is always going to be a panopticon in these scenarios, since the panopticon will be built by whoever controls or pressures the labs.
