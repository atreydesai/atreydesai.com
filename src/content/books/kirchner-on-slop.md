I am not willingly reading slop, but alas AutoFiction has brought me here. Some good ideas that condense a lot of what I saw as anecdotal evidence in Reddit communities and more academic publications, but ultimately not relevant. This is tested only on one paragraph and I assume that long structures or more complexities, such as introducing dialogue, are going to tank the preference of the LLM-generated material.

"I think that is pretty decent. The writing is a bit flat, but that seems preferable to the sloppy default. We can always add character later."

OK, how though? At that point, you might as well co-write with the LLM for significantly better results, at significantly lower price points.
